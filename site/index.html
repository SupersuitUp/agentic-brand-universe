6Why it stays on brand

Canon is the source of truth. Everything else is a projection.

  1. 1
    References carry identityEvery character, setting and motif resolves to a blessed reference, or the render fails loudly.
  2. 2
    Goldens carry tasteBlessed outputs anchor every new render. Taste is captured once.
  3. 3
    Provenance on every outputEvery work knows its canon, its references and its model.
  4. 4
    Canon lives in gitEvery change is a commit. Any past campaign is a checkout.
  5. 5
    A deterministic prompt compilerPrompts are assembled from canon, so a rule cannot be silently dropped.
  6. 6
    A load-bearing render gateNothing renders until its references resolve and its checks pass.
  7. 7
    The kit refuses no-gos in code newOff-palette colours and recorded no-gos fail the build, on every surface that runs the check.

8The spec

Six layers, written down and versioned.

QualityTaste gates, craft canon and provenance, across every layer.
ComposerThe agentic layer, one per form: plans, compiles, generates, repairs.
WorkOne made thing: canon given form.
FormWhat makes a work its kind: a storybook, a flyer, a share card.
GoldensLocked references, passed to the model rather than described to it.
CanonTyped entities and relations in git. The universe itself.
